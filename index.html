Doodlehug Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

中文版本

Doodlehug (“the game”, “we”) is a letter and number tracing game for children aged about 5 to 13, on iPhone, iPad and Android. It is made by ZenBa. This policy explains what information is involved when your child plays.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and nothing a child types or draws ever leaves the device.
  • We don’t run servers and we never receive your child’s data.
  • Ads are set up for children: no personalised ads, no tracking, G-rated only.

What the game itself stores

  • Progress and settings (stars, coins, stickers, best times, crayons, Dot’s outfits, the chosen age group and language, sound and voice settings) are saved only on the device. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • We never ask for a name, email, photo, contacts or location. The game does not use the camera or the microphone.
  • The voice that reads letters and numbers is the device’s own built-in speech. It works offline; no audio or text is sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Because the game is made for children, every ad request is marked as child-directed (under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for Europe and the UK). This means:

  • No personalised or interest-based ads, and no remarketing.
  • No advertising identifier: the game does not ask to track on iPhone or iPad, and the Android Advertising ID permission is removed from the app.
  • Only ads rated suitable for general audiences (G) may be shown.

To deliver ads, limit how often they repeat and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information: IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information (model, OS version, language), and which ads were shown or tapped. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How ads appear

  • Ads never appear while a child is tracing or while a letter comes alive. A full-screen ad may appear only between items, never on the first launch, and no more than one every few minutes.
  • A child can choose to watch an ad to double the coins from a finished item, to get free coins in Dot’s Shop (a few times a day) or to unlock a magic crayon. This is always optional; coins are also earned just by playing, and nothing in the game needs an ad.

For parents

  • Settings, links that leave the game, and ad privacy choices sit behind a grown-up check (a multiplication question).
  • There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and can never be bought with money.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information about your child has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Doodlehug 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日

Doodlehug 是一款为约 5 至 13 岁儿童设计的字母与数字描写游戏，适用于 iPhone、iPad 和 Android，由 ZenBa 制作。

简要说明

  • 没有账号、无需登录、没有聊天功能，孩子输入或描画的内容不会离开设备。
  • 我们不运行服务器，也不会收到孩子的任何数据。
  • 广告已按儿童标准设置：不投放个性化广告、不追踪、只显示适合所有年龄（G 级）的广告。

游戏本身保存的内容

  • 星星、金币、贴纸、最佳用时、蜡笔、Dot 的装扮、年龄组、语言及声音设置等只保存在设备上。删除游戏即删除这些数据。
  • 我们从不索取姓名、邮箱、照片、通讯录或位置，游戏不使用摄像头或麦克风。
  • 朗读字母和数字的语音来自设备自带的语音功能，离线运行，不会发送任何音频或文字。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏免费，并显示 Google AdMob 提供的广告。所有广告请求都标记为面向儿童（符合美国 COPPA）以及未达同意年龄（适用于欧洲和英国），因此：

  • 不投放个性化或基于兴趣的广告，不进行再营销。
  • 不使用广告标识符：在 iPhone 和 iPad 上不会请求追踪权限，Android 版已移除广告 ID 权限。
  • 只显示 G 级（适合所有人）的广告。

为投放广告、控制重复次数及防止欺诈，Google 仍可能处理有限的技术信息：IP 地址（用于大致位置）、设备和应用信息（型号、系统版本、语言），以及显示或点击了哪些广告。

  • 孩子描写时或字母“活起来”时绝不会出现广告；全屏广告只会在两个内容之间出现，首次打开时不会出现，并且间隔数分钟以上。
  • 孩子可以自愿观看广告来让完成后的金币翻倍、在 Dot 的小店领取免费金币（每天几次）或解锁魔法蜡笔。完全可选：玩游戏本身就能获得金币，游戏中没有任何内容必须观看广告。

给家长

  • 设置、离开游戏的链接以及广告隐私选项都需要通过家长验证（一道乘法题）。
  • 游戏内没有任何付费购买。金币只能通过玩游戏获得，不能用钱购买。
  • 我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如您认为孩子的信息被我们获取，请联系我们，我们会将其删除。
