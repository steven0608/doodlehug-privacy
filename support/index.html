Common questions

How do I change the age group, language or path width?

On Home, tap the gear icon and answer the grown-up question. Settings has Player (Little 5–8 or Big 9–13), Language (English or 中文), Path width, Sound, Voice and Vibration.

My child's finger keeps leaving the line.

Choose a wider path in Settings. There is no way to fail: when a finger wanders, the line simply waits until it comes back. Start at the green dot and follow Dot the firefly.

There's no voice.

Check that Voice is on in Settings and the device isn't muted. Doodlehug uses the device's own speech; on iPhone and iPad you can add better voices in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

How do coins work? Can they be bought?

Coins are earned only by playing: every letter or number finished earns some, the Letter of the Day pays double, and finishing a whole set pays a bonus. Coins can never be bought with money, and there are no in-app purchases. Spend them in Dot's Shop on outfits, trails, magic crayons and Sticker World places.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Doodlehug free. They are set up for children: never personalised, G-rated only, never while tracing, and full-screen ads only appear between items, a few minutes apart. Watching an ad for extra coins or a magic crayon is always optional.

How do I start over?

Settings › Start over clears stars, coins and stickers on this device. Progress is stored only on the device, so deleting the app also removes it.

Privacy

No accounts, no chat, nothing a child draws leaves the device. Read the privacy policy.

常见问题

怎样更改年龄组、语言或描线宽度？

在首页点击齿轮图标并回答家长问题。设置中可以选择玩家（小朋友 5–8 岁或大朋友 9–13 岁）、语言（英语或中文）、描线宽度、声音、语音和震动。

孩子的手指总是偏离线条。

在设置中选择更宽的描线。游戏不会失败：手指偏了，线条就会等它回来。从绿色起点开始，跟着萤火虫 Dot 描。

没有语音。

请确认设置中的语音已打开，设备没有静音。Doodlehug 使用设备自带的语音；在 iPhone 和 iPad 上可以在 设置 › 辅助功能 › 朗读内容 › 声音 中下载更好的中文语音。

金币怎么获得？可以花钱买吗？

金币只能通过玩游戏获得：每完成一个字母或数字都有金币，今日字母金币翻倍，完成一整组还有奖励。金币不能用钱购买，游戏内没有任何付费内容。金币可以在 Dot 的小店购买装扮、轨迹、魔法蜡笔和贴纸世界场景。

为什么有广告？

广告让 Doodlehug 保持免费。广告按儿童标准设置：不个性化、只显示 G 级、描字时绝不出现，全屏广告只在两个内容之间出现且间隔数分钟。观看广告获得金币或魔法蜡笔完全自愿。

怎样重新开始？

设置 › 重新开始 会清除本设备上的星星、金币和贴纸。进度只保存在设备上，删除应用也会删除进度。

隐私

没有账号、没有聊天，孩子描画的内容不会离开设备。请阅读隐私政策。
